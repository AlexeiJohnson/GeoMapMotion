GeoMapMotion

GeoMapMotion is a private desktop application that prepares animated map videos and publishes them to the owner's YouTube channel GeoMapMotion.

Watch GeoMapMotion on YouTube

What the GeoMapMotion app does

The app runs only on its owner's own computer. It builds educational geography and history videos from public data (Wikipedia lists and tables, Natural Earth maps) and uploads the finished videos to one YouTube channel — the owner's own channel, youtube.com/@GeoMapsMotion. The videos are animated maps such as:

  • country battles decided on a wheel of fortune and by real statistics;
  • two countries compared round by round (economy, population, sport, nature);
  • maps of religions, languages and borders changing over the centuries;
  • empires growing and falling over 2,000 years.

Why it uses Google APIs

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel: it uploads each finished video with its title, description and tags, adds its captions and thumbnail, and reads the channel's ID, title and handle to confirm that the right channel is connected. It does not access any other user's data, and it is not offered to the public — there are no user accounts and no sign-in on this website. How the data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

GeoMapMotion makes maps, flags, charts and text from public data. It does not create, edit, upload or publish images or videos of real people, and it never creates sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery of anyone (no AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery). It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Приложение GeoMapMotion

GeoMapMotion — личное приложение владельца канала: работает на его компьютере, готовит анимированные ролики с картами из открытых данных (Википедия, Natural Earth) и через YouTube Data API публикует их только на его канал GeoMapMotion. Изображений реальных людей и интимного контента приложение не создаёт и не публикует; данные Google не используются для рекламы и обучения моделей. Подробнее — в политике конфиденциальности.
